Source calculation notes

Material consumption and rates come from Cost Details and Price $. Raw material cost feeds scrap (1.5%), fixed direct cost (1.3%), variable direct cost (workbook rate), direct G&A (2.5%) and indirect G&A (1.5%). The margin is applied as cost ÷ (1 − margin), then the unit selling price is rounded as in JAF.

The workbook labels scrap 2% but calculates 1.5%. For PUR, C1 charges adhesion while showing zero adhesion quantity. The demo retains both behaviours. Raw material requirements exclude the separate scrap cost allowance.
